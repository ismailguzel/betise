# Initial pilot — 2026-11-05

This report records the earlier broad-scenario diagnostic. The current explicit-recipe
proposal is in [PRODUCTION_PLAN.md](PRODUCTION_PLAN.md); its counts differ from this pilot.

## Observed results

BeTiSe 0.6.1 was installed from its released wheel into a separate Python 3.11
environment on macOS. The pilot requested two series for each of 34 scenarios in
three length categories: **102 jobs / 204 requested series**. No production dataset
was generated and nothing was uploaded to Zenodo.

| Length | Passed jobs | Passed series | Rows | Parquet bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Short | 31/34 | 62 | 4,671 | 1,531,981 |
| Medium | 34/34 | 68 | 28,106 | 1,978,133 |
| Long | 34/34 | 68 | 407,835 | 7,317,327 |
| Total | 99/102 | 198 | 440,612 | 10,827,441 |

Passed means structural checks passed: finite observations, complete series per
shard, IDs/counts, lengths, ordered time indices, and matching component metadata.
It is not a scientific quality assessment. The two recipes sampled per job do not
cover all categorical variants that a 1,000-series job would exercise. Tiny shards
also have disproportionate metadata overhead; do not extrapolate these bytes
directly to production storage.

## Reproducible blockers

| Job | Error |
|---|---|
| `short__single_seasonality` | `No valid configured period found for single_seasonality.` |
| `short__multiple_seasonality` | `multiple_seasonality needs 2 valid periods, but only [7] are available.` |
| `short__contextual_anomaly` | Same single-seasonality error for its selected base |

The current generator requires at least six cycles. The configured single-seasonal
period is 12, requiring at least 72 observations. The short category includes
lengths 50–71. Multiple seasonality requires two distinct periods, but only period
7 from the configured list `[7, 12, 24, 52]` is usable below length 72. The canonical
combination validator accepts these jobs; numerical parameter feasibility fails
later during generation. The three failures were reproduced on retry.

Do not silently drop these scenarios, clamp all short lengths to 72+, reduce the
cycle requirement, or change seasonal periods without documenting the choice.
The eventual fix needs length-aware parameter validation/generation and regression
tests. The released library has not been changed as part of this preparation.

## Paper requirements verified after receiving the PDF

- Sections 3.1–3.4 support the model/property families represented in the draft.
- Section 3.5 specifies the three length ranges, **1,000 samples/configuration**,
  and equal stationary/non-stationary representation.
- Section 4 includes individual properties and n-tuple combinations, comprehensive
  metadata, Parquet generation and CSV distribution through the original Zenodo record.
- Section 5.1's 14 categories and Section 7.4's eight characteristics refer to
  particular analyses, not an exhaustive list of generation configurations. Section
  7.4 excludes seasonality/contextual anomalies from that analysis, not the dataset.

Consequently the first draft's arbitrary 120,000-way balanced-by-job allocation
was replaced with 1,000 per job in the candidate profile. The initial candidate
would contain 102,000 series before resolving binary balance; it is not a final
release plan and the runner blocks candidate execution while scope is draft.

## Stationarity labels need explicit interpretation

For all 34 medium pilot jobs, both realizations agreed on their `is_stationary`
flag: 11 jobs returned 1, and 23 returned 0. Stationary-flagged cases include the
four plain stationary bases, the four volatility bases, SARMA, ARMA+GARCH and
MA+EGARCH. Thus counting only the `stationary` scenario group would misrepresent
the actual metadata balance.

These are observations from this pilot, not a proof that all realizations have the
same theoretical properties. Conditional heteroskedasticity alone does not imply
non-stationarity. Define the dataset's stationarity convention, audit the sampled
parameters/metadata and then determine the balance allocation. Do not force the
labels merely to hit a desired percentage.
